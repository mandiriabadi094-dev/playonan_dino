3. Pengumpulan dan Pembagian Data: NOL (Zero)

Kami menegaskan dengan sepenuh integritas:

  • Tidak Ada Data Pribadi yang Dikumpulkan: Kami tidak meminta pembuatan akun, tidak ada login media sosial, tidak ada pelacakan GPS lokasi, dan tidak ada akses ke kontak atau galeri Anda.

Untuk memberikan pengalaman bermain yang baik, game ini hanya menyimpan informasi non-pribadi secara lokal di dalam memori perangkat Anda sendiri (offline storage), yaitu:

  • Skor Tertinggi (High Score): Rekor nilai lompatan dino Anda agar Anda bisa melampaui rekor sebelumnya.
  • Pengaturan Suara: Status on/off musik latar (BGM) dan efek suara (SFX) yang Anda pilih.

Data ini tersimpan hanya di memori ponsel Anda dan akan terhapus jika Anda menghapus data aplikasi atau meng-uninstall game.

IMPORTANT NOTICE: Playonan Dino is designed and strictly intended for users aged 13 and above (13+).

  • We do not knowingly market or direct this application to children under the age of 13.

3. Data Collection and Sharing: ZERO

We declare with complete integrity:

  • Zero Personal Data Collected: We do not require registration, accounts, social logins, precise GPS tracking, or access to your contacts or media.
  • Zero Data Shared with Third Parties: Because we do not collect your personal information, we NEVER sell, rent, disclose, or share user data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics brokers.
  • No Invasive Trackers: The game does not embed invasive background tracking SDKs.
4. Local Device Storage

To provide a seamless arcade experience, the game saves minimal non-personal game states exclusively on your local device storage:

  • High Scores: Your best dino score so you can challenge your own records offline.
  • Audio Preferences: Your preferred toggle status for background music (BGM) and sound effects (SFX).

This data never leaves your device and is wiped if you clear the app's cache or uninstall the application.

5. Device Permissions

Playonan Dino does not request sensitive Android/iOS runtime permissions. We do not access your camera, microphone, Bluetooth, files, or precise physical location.
